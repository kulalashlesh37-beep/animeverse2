SYNOPSIS

In a world where magic is everything, Asta is born without an ounce of magical energy. Unyielding in the face of despair, he trains his physical body to its absolute limits. Upon turning fifteen, he receives a mysterious five-leaf grimoire housing an ancient anti-magic demon sword. Joined by his genius foster brother Yuno, Asta vows to shatter all destinies and become the next Wizard King.
